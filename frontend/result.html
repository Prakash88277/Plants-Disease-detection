Identified Plant: Tomato

The tomato is the edible berry of the plant Solanum lycopersicum, commonly known as a tomato plant. The species originated in western South America and Central America.

Detected Issue: Early Blight (Confidence: 96.7%)

Early blight is a disease of tomato caused by the fungus Alternaria tomato. It occurs everywhere tomatoes are grown. It can cause damping-off, collar rot, stem cankers, leaf blight, and fruit rot.

Recommended Action

  • Prune or remove infected leaves immediately.
  • Improve air circulation around the plants.
  • Avoid overhead watering; use drip irrigation.
  • Apply appropriate copper-based fungicides if the condition worsens.
